Hi, I'm Aziz Amadi

Welcome to AZCHIVE. For me, art isn't just about the finished piece—it's about the quiet process of bringing ideas to life through line, shape, and narrative. I genuinely enjoy every stage of creation, whether it's sketching raw character concepts, building detailed vector artwork, or experimenting with comic layout and motion. Design is where my curiosity finds form, and this archive is a collection of the visual stories I love to build.
